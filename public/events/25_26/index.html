Ephemeral Architectures

In the historical city of Naples
AY2025-26 Summer

Architecture of Ephemerality investigates the architectural potential of ephemeral structures within the historical city. Rather than defining ephemerality simply by short duration, the course understands it through temporality, adaptability, appearance, disappearance and transformation. Ephemeral structures are explored as architectural devices that enter into dialogue with existing urban layers, intensify public life for a limited period, and leave social, cultural or perceptual traces beyond their physical presence.

Set in the historical center of Naples, the course examines how temporary interventions engage with the complexity of place without requiring permanent alteration. Particular attention is given to the tectonic and material expression of temporality: how structures are assembled, supported, occupied, modified, dismantled, reused, relocated or remembered. Materiality is treated as a central design question through atmosphere, construction logic, sensory experience and cultural meaning.

Within the framework of a speculative Architecture District Festival, the course frames underused public spaces as sites for temporary architectural propositions. These interventions may operate as shelters, stages, thresholds, installations, gathering devices or urban markers, creating new situations of encounter, participation and collective experience.

Through site analysis, drawing, model-making and material experimentation, the course asks how ephemeral structures can act critically within the historical city: temporary in duration, deliberate in construction, and lasting in their capacity to reshape how public space is perceived and inhabited.
