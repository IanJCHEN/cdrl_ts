Volumetric Textile

Transforming Textile into Architectural Tectonics
AY2024-25 Summer

Line to surface to volume: woven from spun linear threads, textile is a medium that shapes a volume which softly wraps people or objects. This intensive summer elective invites students to rethinki and reinvent the boundaries between textile and architecture, set against the vibrant backdrop of Milan’s renowned textile and design industry. Through developing a fabratiaion based-project, participants will examine how an inquiry into materiality and fabrication processes leads to innovative transformations in spatial design. This course is framed around Gottfried Semper’s seminal theory that draw a connection between weaving and architecture that underpines the intertwining of spatial creation with materiality and craftsmanship. Over the ten days in Milan and through visits to the cities around, participants will learn how seemingly plain elements of textile can be transformed into innovative three-dimensional architectural constructs informed by materiality and fabrication processes.

This oversee elective course is structured around a intensive workshop to develop a project. Studnets will examin the dynamic relationship between structure and ornamentation of a textile by exploring a specific material and fabrication process, then transform the gained knowledge of the properties of the material and fabrication into a formation by satial constructs that stand by themselves. The outcome of the ourse should be making of a prototype and its application in architectural design. The students will work in groups of 3 to 5 people for 10 days in the classroom of PoliMi. The worshop is supplemented by lectures from the profefessors from PoliMi and CUHK, as well as visits to the places of importence for the industry in Milan and nearby cities.
