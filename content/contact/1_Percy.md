#### Introduction

Percy Wong Ying is a Junior Research Assistant at the Craft Design Research Lab, School of Architecture, The Chinese University of Hong Kong. The research focuses on the thermal relaxation of bamboo and heat-bending techniques, as well as the preservation of Hong Kong’s traditional craft knowledge through material experimentation and hands-on prototyping.
